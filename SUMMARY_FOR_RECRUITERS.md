# Summary for Recruiters

**Salesforce Org Operations & Release Management**
*A portfolio project by Nilesh P Raghavan*

---

## In one paragraph

Nilesh designed how a small sales and support team would run on Salesforce. He decided who can see and change what, which rules keep the data clean, what gets automated, what leaders see on dashboards, how changes move safely between Salesforce environments, and how users get help. He built it all as Salesforce configuration files and documentation. He also built a small help assistant that answers only from approved instructions and refuses anything else. In testing it gave the right answer 20 out of 20 times and correctly refused all 10 questions it shouldn't answer.

## Roles this project supports

- Salesforce Administrator

- Salesforce Release or Technical Operations Coordinator

## Honest scope (worth knowing before a screen)

- Built on **free Salesforce Developer Edition** orgs. There is **no production, Enterprise, or sandbox** environment involved.
- The Salesforce configuration is **complete as source files but hasn't been deployed** to an org yet. That's the next step.
- **Lifecycle Center** (Salesforce's release tool) was **studied and planned for**, not installed. **Flosum** access was **not obtained**.
- The help assistant's results come from a fixed 30-question test run locally.

## Why this candidate stands out

- **Thinks like an operations owner.** Writes down who does what before configuring anything.
- **Security-minded.** No role gets "Modify All Data", not even the admin.
- **Careful with claims.** Every document says clearly what was built, what was documented, and what still needs a live org.
- **Builds AI that knows its limits.** The assistant refuses rather than guesses.
